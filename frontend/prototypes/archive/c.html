How it works

Feeds from 73 newsrooms are read every hour. The same event, reported in different languages, is grouped into one story. An AI judge compares two articles about the same story at a time — without seeing the outlets' names — and a Bayesian model turns those verdicts into positions with uncertainty.

Method

Everything is relative: the centre is the average reference outlet, not a political centre. In a country view the centre moves to that country's five largest news brands; the other outlets are drawn hollow. Ovals are uncertainty; trails are history, fading with age.

Data

Aggregates only — article text is never published. Every assumption, prompt and experiment is in the open repository. Hosting costs about three dollars a month.
