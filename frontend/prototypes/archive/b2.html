01

How it works

Every hour BasedNews reads the feeds of the five largest news brands in each of ten countries, plus a few more, and compares how they report the same events.

CollectNews feeds from 73 outlets, every hour. Robots.txt and opt-outs respected.
MatchArticles about the same event are grouped, whatever their language.
CompareAn AI judge sees two articles side by side — never the outlets' names — and picks per axis.
PlaceA Bayesian model turns thousands of verdicts into positions, with ovals of uncertainty.
02

Method

Positions are relative. The centre is the average of the reference outlets, not a political centre. Open a country and its own five largest brands become the centre; its other outlets are drawn hollow.

What is measured is framing of the same event, not which stories an outlet chooses to cover. All prompts, assumptions and experiments are public, and article text is never republished.
